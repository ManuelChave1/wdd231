About Me

I’m Manuel Chave, Mozambican, curious by nature and someone who loves spending good moments with my family and friends. I started learning programming simply because I wanted to know: “How do these websites and programs really work behind the scenes?”

That’s how I entered this world, and honestly, I’m loving it! The more I learn, the more things I discover, and the more I want to keep exploring. I’m still building my path in programming, making mistakes, learning, struggling with bugs, and celebrating every little “Finally, it works!” moment
